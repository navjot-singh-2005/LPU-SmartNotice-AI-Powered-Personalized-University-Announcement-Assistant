⚡ 1-Click Live Browser Extractor (Bypasses Cloudflare)

Because your browser is already authenticated and past Cloudflare, this snippet fetches or scrapes your live notices and pushes them directly into SmartNotice via CORS.

  1. Log into ums.lpu.in in another tab.
  2. Press F12 (or right click -> Inspect) and open the Console tab.
  3. Paste the snippet below and press Enter.
